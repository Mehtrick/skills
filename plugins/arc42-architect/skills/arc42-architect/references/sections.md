# arc42 section map

1. Introduction and goals — purpose, key requirements, quality goals, and stakeholders.
2. Architecture constraints — technical, organizational, and applicable conventions.
3. Context and scope — business and technical boundaries and external interfaces.
4. Solution strategy — the main approaches that address goals and constraints.
5. Building block view — static decomposition, responsibilities, and interfaces.
6. Runtime view — representative interactions and failure paths among building blocks.
7. Deployment view — execution environments, infrastructure, and mapping of building blocks to nodes.
8. Crosscutting concepts — architectural approaches applied in multiple areas.
9. Architecture decisions — significant choices, rationale, and consequences.
10. Quality requirements — quality priorities and measurable scenarios.
11. Risks and technical debt — unresolved risks and known architectural compromises.
12. Glossary — project-specific terms and abbreviations.

Source: [arc42 template overview](https://arc42.org/overview/).
